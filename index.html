How to play

  • Two teams take turns. The other team secretly picks a film for your actor, or the deck deals one.
  • The actor presses Start and acts. Their team shouts guesses. The actor taps Got it! the moment someone's right.
  • Faster guesses score more: 50 points plus the seconds left. Under 20 seconds is a Lightning bonus, and back-to-back hits build a streak.
  • Playing from different places? Turn on typed guesses and the actor camera in the lobby, and use voice chat.
